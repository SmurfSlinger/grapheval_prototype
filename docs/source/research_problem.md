# Research problem

Final-answer string matching hides whether an answer’s internal statements are
supported, contradicted, or unevidenced, and whether revision preserved or erased
correct information.

## Research questions (official report)

1. How reliably does GraphEval produce textually correct and graph-resolved
   answers across designed depths 1–10?
2. How are SUPPORTED / CONTRADICTED / NO_EVIDENCE claims handled during revision?
3. Which failures are primarily model, primarily pipeline, or mixed?
4. What do traces show about preservation, correction, removal, or regression?

## What the experiment does **not** claim

There was no controlled no-feedback or generic-self-correction baseline. Results
support feasibility and case-level evidence only.
